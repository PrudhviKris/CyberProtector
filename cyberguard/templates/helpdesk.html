Need help right now?

If you or someone you know is being bullied online:

  • Don't respond or retaliate. Save screenshots as evidence.
  • Block the person and report them to the platform.
  • Talk to someone you trust: a parent, teacher or friend.
  • In India, report cybercrime at cybercrime.gov.in or call the helpline 1930. For women in distress, call 181.
  • If you are in immediate danger, call 112.
