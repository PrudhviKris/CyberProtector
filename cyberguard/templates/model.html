Ensemble

Final score = 0.4 × baseline + 0.6 × max(transformer labels). The message is flagged at ≥ 0.5. Severity is high at ≥ 0.85 (or when the threat / severe-toxicity scores are high), medium at ≥ 0.70, and low otherwise.

Explainability

Word occlusion: each distinct word is removed in turn, and the drop in the baseline's log-odds is that word's contribution, shown relative to the most influential word.

What changed from v1

  • The v1 notebook duplicated every row (pd.concat([df, df])) before splitting, which leaked test rows into training and inflated accuracy. v2 removes duplicates first.
  • The v1 app called fit_transform on each input, re-fitting the IDF weights on a single sentence. v2 ships one fitted sklearn Pipeline.
  • Added character n-grams and obfuscation decoding, so masked words like f*ck are still caught.
