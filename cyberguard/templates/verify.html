How it works

  1. Text is normalised: lowercase, URLs and @mentions removed, and obfuscations like f*ck or b1tch decoded.
  2. A TF-IDF model (word and character n-grams) and a fine-tuned BERT model each score the text.
  3. The two scores are blended. Messages scoring ≥ 50% are flagged and graded by severity.
  4. A word-occlusion analysis highlights the words that drove the decision.
